Sets, functions & metric spaces

Topological spaces

Open sets as the primitive notion; basis & subbasis; closure, interior and boundary.

 2 Point-setCore

Continuity & constructions

Continuous maps, homeomorphisms, and building new spaces: subspace, product and quotient topologies.

 3 Point-setCore

Connectedness & compactness

The two great topological properties — and what continuous maps do to them.

 4 Point-setIntermediate

Separation, countability & metrization

The T-axioms, countability, Urysohn's lemma and Tietze — leading to when a space is metrizable.

 5 Point-setAdvanced

Convergence & completeness

Nets & filters for general convergence, complete metric spaces, and the Baire category theorem.

 6 AlgebraicAdvanced

Homotopy & the fundamental group

Deforming maps, \(\pi_1\), \(\pi_1(S^1)\cong\mathbb{Z}\), and Seifert–van Kampen.

 7 AlgebraicAdvanced

Covering spaces

Lifting, the Galois-style correspondence with subgroups of \(\pi_1\), and the universal cover.

 8 AlgebraicExpertise

Homology & cohomology

Simplicial/CW complexes, singular homology, exact sequences, Betti numbers and the cup product.
